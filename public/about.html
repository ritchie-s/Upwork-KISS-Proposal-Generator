Why I built KISS Proposal

I was losing Upwork proposals left and right. Clients hate long proposals. I was spending 30 minutes writing something that gets ignored in 3 seconds. It was frustrating.

Then I discovered that clients don't read long proposals. They read the first 2-3 sentences and decide. So I started writing shorter, punchier proposals — and my response rate jumped from 3% to 40%.

I thought: "What if I could help every freelancer do this automatically?"

And that's how KISS Proposal was born. 💕

Built in love. Kept free. Forever.
